Two retrieval worlds. The ontology graph ranks diseases from structured phenotypes; the passage store answers questions in words. Both point at the same identifiers, so every sentence can cite either.
Nothing unverified is shown. The verifier sits between the composer and the clinician; a sentence that fails is dropped and logged, never softened.
Honest numbers. The blind set is frozen before any index exists, scored once, and scored strictly so a patient never meets annotations taken from their own paper.
